# 三经合一 — Three Streams Converge

## Why this skill exists

> **原点只有一个。** There is only one origin.

The question is not "how to improve rankings." The question is: **"When someone searches, is your content their best answer?"**

This skill is built on three philosophical streams:

---

## Laozi — The Way of Water (神 · Spirit)

> **上善若水。** The highest good is like water.
> Water benefits all things without contention. It dwells in places people disdain, therefore it is near to the Tao.
> — *道德经 · 第八章*

Water's qualities, embodied in this skill:

| Quality | In seo-geo-master |
|---------|-------------------|
| **Benefits without contention** | It serves you without demanding you learn commands |
| **Dwells in low places** | It does the tedious work — audits, GSC analysis, content QA — that no one enjoys |
| **Shapeless, formless** | Speak naturally; it adapts to your words, not the other way around |
| **Dripping water pierces stone** | One PDCA cycle shows little. Ten, a hundred — real SEO is accumulation, not a single burst |

> **大道至简。** The great way is simplicity itself. True power is never complex.

---

## Sun Tzu — The Way of Strategy (骨 · Bone)

> **兵者，国之大事，死生之地，存亡之道，不可不察也。**
> War is the greatest affair of state, the basis of life and death, the Way to survival or extinction. It must be thoroughly pondered and analyzed.
> — *孙子兵法 · 始计篇*

The SEO battlefield, governed by five dimensions:

| 五事 (Five) | Meaning | SEO/GEO mapping |
|-----------|---------|-----------------|
| **道** | Alignment of purpose | Does your content align with the searcher's true intent? |
| **天** | Timing and climate | Industry search cycles, Core Update windows, competitor seasonality |
| **地** | Terrain | Technical foundation: crawlability, indexability, speed, mobile, security |
| **将** | Leadership | Your authority: E-E-A-T signals, author credentials, sources, trust |
| **法** | Systems and discipline | PDCA cadence, audit frequency, fix discipline, safety gates |

> **知己知彼，百战不殆。** Know yourself and know your enemy, and you will never be defeated.
> **先胜而后求战。** First win, then go to war.
> **不战而屈人之兵。** Subdue the enemy without fighting.

---

## Inamori Kazuo — The Way of Humanity (魂 · Soul)

> **作为人，何谓正确？**
> As a person, what is the right thing to do?
> — 稻盛和夫 (Inamori Kazuo)

For SEO/GEO, this means:

- **Someone is searching.** A real person. With real confusion, need, pain. **Help them.**
- **Don't deceive.** No clickbait. No keyword stuffing. No fake authority.
- **Don't waste.** Don't make them click through only to be disappointed. The answer is on your page.
- **Serve others.** Rankings, traffic, AI citations — these are not goals. They are the natural result of genuinely helping people.

> Origin: **以利他之心，做对人有用的内容。** With an altruistic heart, create content that truly helps people.

## Three core doctrines

### 1. First win, then go to war (先胜而后求战)
Before publishing, the content itself must already be good enough to win. Don't rely on post-publish patching.

### 2. Subdue without fighting (不战而屈人之兵)
The best ranking battle is the one you win before it starts. Don't compete on "one more H2 than competitors." Compete on the angle they didn't think of.

### 3. Speed is essential (兵贵胜，不贵久)
Don't let one PDCA cycle drag on for weeks. `audit_only` mode eliminates decision paralysis. Fix by priority: impact × ease, high first.
